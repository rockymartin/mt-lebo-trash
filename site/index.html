Frequently asked questions

How do I find my trash day in Mt. Lebanon, PA?

Search your street name above. Collection day is assigned per street in the borough’s schedule.

When is recycling picked up?

Recycling is on the same weekday as trash, every other week. The calendar marks recycling weeks.

What about holidays?

Weekday holidays usually push pickup back one day for the rest of that week. The calendar reflects common holiday rules.

Is this official?

No — it’s a community tool using Mt. Lebanon Public Works data. Verify with the municipality when needed.
